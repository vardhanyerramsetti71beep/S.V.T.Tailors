## 🌟 Key Features & Lively Experiences

1. **Lively Tailor Boutique Design**:
   - Royal navy, atelier gold, and parchment ivory theme reflecting 6 decades of bespoke tailoring.
   - Interactive hero showcase with real photography, trust badges, and floating status.
2. **Interactive Garment Customizer & Price Estimator ("Design Your Fit")**:
   - Choose garment (Suits, Shirts, Trousers, Wedding Sherwani, Designer Blouse, Safari Suits, Uniforms, Alterations).
   - Real-time price calculation combining stitching + fabric + express speed options.
   - Custom styling: Collar options, Cuffs, Lapels, Fit profiles (Slim, Classic, Executive).
   - 4 Flexible Measurement Methods:
     * Standard S/M/L/XL sizing.
     * Custom body inches (Chest, Waist, Shoulder, Sleeve, Inseam).
     * Replicate an existing sample garment sent to the workshop.
     * Request free Master Tailor Doorstep visit in Vijayawada.
3. **Easy Multi-Path Ordering System**:
   - **One-Click WhatsApp Order**: Formats an order message with itemized pricing, measurements, and address, directly opening a chat with `+91 98481 33417`.
   - **Digital Receipt & Invoice**: Displays an interactive modal with barcode simulation, order ID, breakdown, printable invoice, and tracking shortcut.
   - **Order Bag (Cart Drawer)** with localStorage persistence.
4. **Interactive Visual Body Measurement Guide**:
   - Clickable mannequin silhouette with hotspots for Neck, Shoulder, Chest, Sleeve, Waist, and Inseam, explaining exactly how to measure for a sharp fit.
5. **Vijayawada Doorstep Measurement & Fabric Pickup**:
   - Localities covered: Governorpet, Arundalpet, Benz Circle, MG Road, Suryaraopet, Gunadala, Moghalrajpuram, Satyanarayanapuram, Bhavanipuram, Penamaluru, Poranki, etc.
6. **Live Atelier Order Tracker**:
   - Real-time simulation of garment progress: `Order Logged` ➔ `Fabric Cut` ➔ `Master Stitch` ➔ `Steam Press` ➔ `Ready for Pickup / Dispatched`.
7. **Google Maps Integration**:
   - Direct interactive directions button and map embed pointing directly to `https://maps.app.goo.gl/tUgrhQhHEAkgGBXQ7`.
