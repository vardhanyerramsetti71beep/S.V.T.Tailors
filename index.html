How To Order Your Bespoke Fit

We make custom gents tailoring effortless, transparent, and accessible to every patron in three easy steps.

Step 1

Choose Your Style & Cloth

Select a suit, safari jacket, shirt, trouser, or sherwani. You can provide your own cloth or choose from our premium fabric selections.

Step 2

Simple Sizing & Measurements

Choose standard sizes (36-46), type your custom body inches, send a best-fitting sample garment, or book our free doorstep home visit!

Step 3

Master Cut & Delivered

Collar & Neck Measurement

Wrap the measuring tape around the base of your neck where your shirt collar rests. Allow one finger space between the tape and neck for comfort.

Master Tailor Tip:

For stiff formal Italian collars, keep exact snug measurement. For casual kurtas, add 0.5 inches.
